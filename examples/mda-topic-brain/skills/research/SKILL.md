# Topic research

1. Restate the question as concrete claims to verify.
2. Read relevant files under `/memories/agent/` and note stale or conflicting knowledge.
3. Search the web. Prefer primary sources, record publication dates, and corroborate consequential claims.
4. Search available read-only internal connectors when internal context would improve the answer.
5. Compare sources, resolve contradictions where possible, and label remaining uncertainty.
6. Answer with direct citations and separate public evidence from internal context.
7. Save only durable, broadly shareable conclusions to memory, with source links, dates, and confidence.
